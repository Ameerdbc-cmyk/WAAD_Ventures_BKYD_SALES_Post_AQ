# WAAD_Ventures_BKYD_SALES_POST_ACQUISITION

#Overview

This project analyzes restaurant sales performance following a change in ownership, focusing on March–April operations. The goal was to evaluate overall performance and identify opportunities to increase revenue through operational improvements and menu optimization.

The analysis was conducted using SQL for data processing and Tableau for visualization, resulting in a business-focused dashboard and recommendations.

# Objective

Evaluate post-acquisition performance and identify actionable opportunities to increase revenue through:

Upselling improvements
Menu optimization
Operational consistency

#Key Insights
Revenue remained stable (-0.3%) despite Ramadan and reduced operating hours, indicating strong baseline demand
Revenue is highly concentrated, with ~80% generated by a small subset of items and ~50% driven by BBQ platters
Desserts (0.8%) and modifiers (0.6%) contribute minimally, revealing significant missed high-margin opportunities

Post-acquisition franchise training likely contributed to stable performance; however, upselling behaviors (particularly for desserts) remain inconsistently applied, indicating an opportunity to standardize and scale these practices across all staff

# Analysis
Cleaned and structured POS (Clover) data using Python & SQL
Aggregated data at category and item levels in SQL
Built comparison tables for March vs April performance
Developed a Tableau dashboard to visualize trends, concentration, and opportunities
Presented a PPT to stakeholders at all levels 

# Recommendations
1. Standardize Upselling Across Staff
Train servers to consistently suggest desserts and add-ons
Introduce structured prompts (e.g., asking about dessert when clearing plates)

2. Increase Dessert Attachment Rate
Promote high-performing desserts (e.g., lava cake)
Bundle desserts with popular platters

3. Optimize Menu and Inventory
Reevaluate underperforming items (e.g., mushroom-based dishes, fried chicken)
Adjust offerings to better align with demand

# Expected Impact
Targeted improvements in upselling execution and menu optimization present a clear opportunity to generate incremental revenue with minimal additional cost.

Dessert Attach Rate
Increase from 0.8% → 5%
→ Estimated +$300–$600 per day
(Based on ~$8 average dessert and increased customer conversion)
Modifier Revenue
Increase from 0.6% → 2%
→ Estimated +$1K–$3K per month
(Low-cost add-ons with near 100% margin)
Menu Efficiency
Streamlined inventory and reduced waste
Improved focus on high-performing, high-margin items
Better inventory turnover and operational simplicity

Even modest improvements in upselling behavior can generate meaningful revenue growth due to the high-margin nature of desserts and modifiers.
